Request account data deletion

You can request deletion of data associated with your Fomopad wallet account. Open the app, sign in with that wallet, and submit a request. The signature proves control of the public wallet; it does not spend SOL.

Open account data controls →

What a request covers

The Fomopad operator reviews requests to remove private off-chain account information, active sessions, and unused draft data where applicable. This is a deletion request, not a transaction to close or transfer your wallet. The request is recorded for the operator to process.

What may remain

Solana transactions, mint addresses, public fee receipts, published launch metadata, and information needed for transaction integrity, security, disputes, or legal obligations may remain. Blockchain records cannot be erased. A request does not burn tokens, move funds, change creator fee ownership, or reverse a launch.

Cannot sign in?

Contact @fomoxpad for help. Provide only your public wallet address. Never share a seed phrase or private key.

Privacy notice

Effective 26 October 2026

This notice covers the Fomopad website and Android app. Fomopad is an independent Solana interface operated by the Fomopad team. The Android app opens the same live service through a Trusted Web Activity. It needs an internet connection.

Information processed

When you connect or sign in, we process your public Solana wallet address, a signed authentication challenge, and a session token. We process coin names, tickers, descriptions, uploaded artwork, and any website or social links you choose to submit. Launch records, public blockchain transactions, wallet eligibility proofs, and fee receipts are processed to operate the service. Hosting and network providers may process IP addresses, browser information, timestamps, and error logs.

Wallet keys

Fomopad does not ask for your wallet seed phrase or store your wallet private key. Your external wallet approves messages and transactions. Separately, the service generates token mint signing keys, encrypts them on the server, and uses them for the intended launch. A token mint signing key is different from the private key to your connected wallet.

How information is used

Information is used for wallet sign-in, token launches, public token discovery, fee routing, reward eligibility, on-chain receipt verification, abuse prevention, service operation, and support. Public launch information and transaction receipts may be visible to anyone. Uploaded coin images, names, descriptions, links, and wallet addresses should be treated as public when a launch is published.

External services

The service uses Railway hosting and a Postgres database, Solana RPC and blockchain services, Stonk.fun and Meteora integrations, and market data from DEX Screener and GeckoTerminal. Google Fonts and esm.sh may serve web resources. Phantom or Solflare handles wallet approval when you choose it. Those services receive the information needed for requests and have their own privacy notices. The Android package does not embed advertising or analytics SDKs, but that does not mean the live website processes no data.

Storage and security

Session tokens, your watchlist, and app preferences may be stored in your browser storage. Communication with the production website uses HTTPS. Session records expire and are checked by the service. Network and blockchain services have their own security and retention practices. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Deletion and retention

Use Data deletion to request deletion of your Fomopad account data. The service records a request after you sign in with the relevant wallet. The Fomopad operator reviews requests and can remove private off-chain account data, sessions, and unused drafts where applicable. Public blockchain records cannot be erased. Published launch metadata and records needed for security, transaction integrity, dispute handling, or legal obligations may be retained. You may also clear local browser storage and disconnect your wallet.

Audience and contact

The service is intended for adults aged 18 and over. For privacy questions or help with a deletion request, contact the Fomopad team through @fomoxpad. An operator must verify a request before deleting data. Never send a seed phrase or private key.

Updates

Material changes will be reflected in this notice with an updated effective date.
